Project context

Ordering from several local shops in one checkout

SoldOut is an earlier, co-developed university project that connects customers with local vendors and the drivers who deliver for them. One cart can hold products from several markets, and the app covers browsing through to delivery.

It predates my client work, and it’s here partly to show where I started: a GetX app with a Clean Architecture folder structure, REST integration, and a bilingual UI.

My role

Flutter Developer on a university team project. Other teammates owned parts of the app I don’t list here.

The screens and flows I worked on:

  • Product details and market details.
  • Cart and orders.
  • Addresses, payment methods, and favorites.
  • Customer- and driver-facing screens, in Arabic and English.

Project-level challenge

One sensible route instead of stops in random order

A driver carrying orders from several vendors shouldn’t visit them in whatever order they happened to be assigned. The driver flow uses OSRM to optimize multi-stop routing, then draws the route on an OpenStreetMap layer with flutter_map while geolocation tracks the driver.

The routing was a team feature; my part of the driver experience was the screens around it.

Limitations and next improvement

Earlier project; networking would be one of the first areas I would rebuild with typed failures and retry handling — the pattern my later apps use.
