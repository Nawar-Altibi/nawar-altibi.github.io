04Approach

How I build

Three engineering habits that shape how I approach real product work.

01

Architecture that survives change

I separate presentation, domain, and data concerns early, and keep failure states explicit. When an API contract changes, the fix should stay close to the layer that owns it.

Seen in: Qeyadah · Mawaqef

02

Arabic and English from the first screen

RTL, localization, dates, currency, and error states are part of the product from the beginning — not a cleanup task after the UI is finished.

Seen in: Client and team projects

03

The unhappy path is part of the feature

A booking that conflicts after a dropped connection, a deposit that must expire, or sensitive data that should never be readable on the server. I design the failure path as deliberately as the happy path.

Seen in: Qeyadah · Mawaqef · Shifaa
